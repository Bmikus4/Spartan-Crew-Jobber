# 2026-10-28 The system measures itself against staff's actions; five rulings

**Decided by:** the owner, after the Part 1 audit (ADDENDUM §12).

**The purpose, in the owner's words:** "Every urgent decision for Spartan on the TV, by reading their email and
syncing with OnSinch. Create and update eventually fully automatic. Nothing automatic goes to OnSinch until live data
proves it's accurate. A person's Confirm always books." The bar is 1 failed order in 100, measured against what staff
do in OnSinch: "That is the only ground truth." No model labels, no judging by the agent.

**Rulings.**
1. **A moved start keeps the end.** "The start moves, the end stays. Revise the code to match my ruling." A new start
   at or after the old end makes no zero or negative shift: it goes to staff with a plan and a Confirm. The loop keeps
   counting what staff do on moved starts, so the ruling and staff's edits can be compared. (Before this ruling staff
   kept the length on 11 of 12 start-only requests, one mixed, none kept the end: ADDENDUM §12.1.)
2. **The two-week rule covers changes:** "Every change within 14 days goes to staff, with a plan and a Confirm. I
   understand this means no automatic changes for now." The window, and whether it covers creates, changes or both, is
   a setting.
3. **Nothing is hard-wired to a person.** "Every hand-off carries the system's best plan and a Confirm, and a person's
   Confirm always books." The plan each rule held back is recorded for every rule-based hand-off. "Day already over"
   stays a card.
4. **One order per venue; at one venue, one order across days and weeks.** Orders that break it are flagged and
   categorised (ADDENDUM §12, exceptions protocol). Venue accuracy on creates is first on the builder's list.
5. **Measurement first:** the loop, a 5-7 day baseline, then the rule changes one at a time. "Where it's cheap,
   shadow-score both versions of a rule on the same emails."

**Earlier today, with the audit request:** a new client's first order is allowed; "yes, book that" drafts and hands
off.
